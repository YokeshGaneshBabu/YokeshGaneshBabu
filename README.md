- Eight-band tuning architecture
- Frequency, phase-noise and power evaluation

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Ultra-Low-Power Wake-Up Receiver</strong></summary>

A 2.4 GHz OOK receiver front-end exploration involving envelope detection, a behavioral comparator and digital synchronization logic.

- Subthreshold envelope detection
- Verilog-A behavioral modeling
- Digital synchronization logic

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Back-Gated FinFET Characterization</strong></summary>

TCAD investigation of device characteristics and back-gate coupling.

- Transfer and output characteristics
- Threshold-voltage shifts and subthreshold swing
- Buried-oxide thickness and back-gate bias analysis

</details>
